## what i know/learning

i've immersed myself in artificial intelligence through hands-on experimentation, project development, and architectural learning. my foundational and growing ai expertise includes:

- **large language models (llms)** — gpt (openai), claude (anthropic), open-source models like mistral, phi, csm-1b.
- **prompt engineering** — crafting reusable, reliable, and chainable prompts.
- **langchain & crewai** — for agentic workflows, tool usage, and chaining.
- **openai api, ollama, huggingface** — integration, fine-tuning, local inference.
- **fastapi & python** — building lightweight genai apis and backends.
- **vector databases** — pinecone, faiss, chromadb for rag systems.
- **aws/gcp/azure** — deploying cloud-native ai services.
