**Key Takeaways:**

- Obsession can be destructive — Captain Ahab’s single-minded pursuit of the whale leads to tragedy  
- Nature is vast and indifferent — the sea and the whale symbolize forces beyond human control  
- Friendship and loyalty matter — Ishmael’s observations highlight human connections amid adversity  
- Perspective shapes understanding — different characters offer varied views of the same events  

> *“Call me Ishmael.”*
