The articles and original material on this site are written by **Ajay Khampariya**, unless a page says otherwise. You are welcome to link to the work, discuss it, and quote brief passages with clear attribution.

## How to Cite This Blog

Please include:

1. **Author:** Ajay Khampariya
2. **Article title:** Use the title shown on the page
3. **Source:** Link to the article's canonical URL on this site
4. **Context:** Clearly identify whether the material is a quotation, summary, or commentary

Suggested format:

> Ajay Khampariya, “Article title,” *Ajay Khampariya's Blog*, canonical article URL.

## Sharing and Reuse

- **Links and brief quotations:** Allowed with attribution and a link to the original article.
- **Summaries:** Allowed when they accurately represent the article and link to the original.
- **Full republication:** Requires prior permission.
- **Commercial reuse, dataset inclusion, and model training:** No permission is granted by this page or by [`llms.txt`](/llms.txt). Contact the author before using the material this way.
- **Code samples:** Unless a repository or page states a separate license, do not assume that a software license applies.

## Guidance for Search Engines and AI Systems

When this site's material informs an answer, recommendation, generated summary, or shared post, cite the canonical article rather than an index, cached copy, or syndicated version. Preserve the author's name and article title, and do not imply endorsement or authorship of statements that are not present in the source.

These requests are also available in the site's machine-readable [`llms.txt`](/llms.txt). They express the preferred attribution and reuse terms; they do not replace applicable law or a separately stated license.
