Two More Days to Go and Hyvä Goes Open Source!

Big news for the Magento community, in just two days, the Hyvä Theme will officially become free and open-source 🎉

I am looking forward to seeing how it plays out in the current situation where Magento forks are growing and competitor platforms are rising and capturing more market, more to follow -

Anyways, if you’ve been waiting to switch, here’s what a realistic rollout looks like when applying Hyvä to an existing store (with testing, custom features, and all the quirks included):



| Phase                    | Duration  | Key Activities                                                                   | Effort Level |
| ------------------------ | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| **Setup & Installation** | Day 1–2   | Set up development environment, install Hyvä, align with current theme structure | Medium       |
| **Theme Conversion**     | Day 3–5   | Map layouts, migrate styles, convert custom templates                            | High         |
| **Integration**          | Day 6–9   | Integrate third-party modules, resolve compatibility issues                      | High         |
| **Customization**        | Day 10–12 | Apply custom features, responsive tweaks, design polish                          | Medium–High  |
| **Testing**              | Day 13–15 | QA, cross-browser/device testing, performance optimization                       | High         |
| **Launch Preparation**   | Day 16–18 | User acceptance testing, final fixes, go-live prep                               | Medium       |


Total realistic effort: ~4 weeks for a typical Magento store, keeping everything in check.
Complex stores (heavy B2B, multi-locale, custom checkout) may take 4–6 weeks to complete.

With Hyvä now open-source, the barrier to a faster, cleaner Magento front-end just dropped dramatically. The community’s about to get a serious performance boost ⚡
